The 7-Night Lucid Dream Experiment

A guided week of science-tested techniques, each labeled with how strong the evidence really is. Lucid dreaming is a skill, not a switch: many people have one in their first week and many don't. Either way, you end the week with a record of your own dreams and a clear idea of what to try next.

  • The Guide (PDF, ~25 pages): 7 nights, one technique per night (dream journaling, dream signs, reality checks, MILD, optional wake-back-to-bed, SSILD, your best combination), each with steps, why it might work, an evidence label (Strong / Moderate / Preliminary / Hypothesis) and its limits.
  • 7 short audio sessions (3–5 min each, MP3): one calm guide per night, to play at bedtime.
  • The Morning Log (printable PDF): a checkbox-first page that takes 60 seconds right after waking, plus a Day 7 review page.
  • What the research actually says: in the largest field study (Aspy, 2020; 355 people who finished), lucid nights went from about 5% to about 16% in one practice week — an average, and many participants had none. No technique works for everyone.
  • Safety page: this is education, not medical advice. Never trade real sleep for a technique; the two wake-up nights are optional; what sleep paralysis is and what to do. Not for people with insomnia, a sleep disorder, frequent distressing nightmares or sleep paralysis, or short sleep from shift work — talk to a professional first.
